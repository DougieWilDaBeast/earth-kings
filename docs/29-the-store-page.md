# 29 — The store page (draft)

**A draft, not a page.** Nothing here is published, and every word is **Inferred**: a working draft
for the founders (agenda item 93). It is written from the First Hour as it plays
today ([28](28-the-first-hour.md)) so that the page can only promise what the demo shows
([D148](06-decisions.md), the designers' fortnight's F5). When the Steam page is made (the retreat's
plan, [D100](06-decisions.md): once the hour plays and the music is clean), it starts from here.

**The rule.** A line or a shot goes on the page only if a new player meets it in the First Hour. If
the hour changes so that something below is no longer true, the line goes in the same pull request.

## Name and one line

**Earth Kings.** Sixteen fell. One climbs.

## Five lines (the short description)

1. Sixteen demigods fell into sixteen copies of Earth and forgot who they were. You are one of them.
2. Walk a hostile land to the Tower at its centre, and fight what finds you on the ground where you
   meet it, in real time, pausing when you choose.
3. Climb the Tower floor by floor; on the fifth, something you lost is waiting, and it is yours alone.
4. When your lead dies, their world ends for good, and the next of the sixteen begins in their own.
5. The First Hour is free: the crater, the Tower's city, the Guild, the first gate and five floors.

Each line, and where a player meets it in the hour: 1 the opening ([25](25-the-opening.md)); 2 the
road and its bands ([D98](06-decisions.md)); 3 the floors and the chapter ones
([D102](06-decisions.md), [D103](06-decisions.md)); 4 the death screen, Ascend and the quiz
([D105](06-decisions.md), [D110](06-decisions.md)); 5 the route in [28](28-the-first-hour.md).
