# 21 — The Sixteen: a first draft

**Nothing on this page is settled.** It is a draft of all sixteen characters for both founders to
keep, change or throw out. Only a founder settles a character. Once a character is confirmed, it
moves into [13 — Heroes & Tempers](13-heroes-and-tempers.md) and is built as the bundle described
there. Until then this page is the proposal.

It is written from two sources:

- **[Joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md)** (2026-09-24): what
  the roster has to do, the four spoken names, and five seeds.
- **Fifteen more seeds from `dougie`** (2026-09-26): characters from manga, manhwa and anime, each
  sent with a summary of their personality, their story and one core trait. They are listed in
  [13](13-heroes-and-tempers.md#seeds-from-dougies-list).

It follows the order `dougie` gave in the session: work out who the characters are first, then fit
each one to a temper.

> "after we decide on what these 16 characters do or just how many storylines we want, it'll
> actually find which personality tests they might be best suited for"
> — dougie, 2026-09-24, session 1 [00:04:39]

A seed shapes a character; it is not the character. Each of the sixteen takes a drive and a way of
behaving from one or two seeds, and gets everything else from the world: the fall, the lost
memory, the Tower and the mark.

Why each session seed went to each temper:

- **The carried mage → INTJ.** A man who has already decided how everything ends, and who cannot
  walk there without someone carrying him.
- **Pain → INFJ.** He is widely read as INFJ: a quiet, certain vision of how the world should be,
  and whatever it takes to get it there.
- **Sung Jin-Woo → ISTJ.** He does the same daily work until he is the strongest. The Ledger counts
  every gain.
- **Luffy → ESFP.** He lives for the moment, and freedom is the whole point of him.
- **Thorfinn → ISTP.** Quiet, all practical skill, and aimed at one person.

`dougie`'s fifteen were placed so that each one's core trait lands on the temper whose four letters
it already shows: Nagi's indifference on the Inward, Sky-read and Loose INTP; Sanji's hospitality on
the Full Table; Hansu's rule-reading on the Straight Road.

## The NT sitting — the first four to build

This is the first sitting on the [roadmap](05-roadmap.md). These four are drafted in the most detail
so they can be built as soon as they are confirmed.

### Atlas — The Long Plan (INTJ)

_Seeds: the carried mage, Kim Dokja._

Named for the Titan who held up the sky. This one cannot hold up himself. He is small and cannot
walk properly, so someone has to carry him. When he casts, nothing else on the field comes close.
People look down on him, often literally.

He behaves like a man who has read to the end of the story. He knows where a gate will open before
it does, and which of the people around him will betray him. He steers people towards the ending
he wants without telling them what it is. His weakness is the one the seed brings: **he values
everyone else above himself.** When they call him "the Baggage", he agrees with them, and he will
spend himself for his bearer without a second thought.

**Inferred, for the founders:** the fall took his memory like everyone else's, but not quite all of
it. What is left comes back as knowing how things end, not what happened. This depends on the open
memory question ([agenda item 6](worldbuilding/answers.md#what-is-still-to-decide)).

- **Fight — Borne.** Passive: Atlas has no move of his own. He moves only when a companion is
  carrying him, and if that companion falls, he is stuck where they dropped him. His four moves are
  the longest-range and slowest-casting in the game. Losing your bearer is how Atlas loses.
- **Party:** he always needs one person, the bearer. He is never alone, and he is never free. Who
  carries him is the character's real question: someone hired, someone loyal, or someone who owes
  him. Placeholder body: `lizard_wizard` ("Tiny blind wizard riding a lizard"), who is already being
  carried.
- **What he tells you:** exactly what you need for the next step, and never the ending.
- **Earned name:** "the Baggage", given by people who dislike him and repeated by Atlas.
- **Difficulty:** 4.

### Janus — The Open Question (INTP)

_Seed: Seishiro Nagi._

The god of doorways and of beginnings and endings. Janus may be the most talented of the sixteen,
and he could not care less. He finds almost everything too much effort. He sleeps in whatever gate
town he landed near, turns down every party that asks, and lets the Guild's rank board go by.

Then a gate opens that nobody understands, and a different Janus gets up and walks into it. He does
not care about winning. He cares about the question, and he stays for as long as it stays hard.

- **Fight — Doorways.** Passive: the more the odds are against his side, the faster his moves come
  back. His moves swap two units, pull an ally out of trouble, and send an enemy into a bad spot. He
  does very little damage himself; he wins fights by putting everyone where he wants them. In an
  easy fight he is the worst of the sixteen, and in a hopeless one he is the best.
- **Party:** only joins when someone brings him a problem worth the walk, and drifts off when it is
  solved. Placeholder body: `timeless_one` ("The all knowing man who stands at the end of time").
- **What he tells you:** whatever he finds interesting, whether or not you asked.
- **Earned name:** "the Sleeper", until the day the Guild starts calling him "the Gatewalker".
- **Difficulty:** 3.

### Bellona — The Marshal (ENTJ)

_Seed: Ash, from I Became the Tyrant of a Defense Game._

The Roman goddess of war, and one of the four names spoken in the session. Bellona lands in a
border fortress that everyone knows will fall. A gate is opening beyond its wall, and the garrison
has been left to die there. Within a week she is commanding it.

She makes hard decisions quickly, and she knows people will die of them. She is also afraid the whole
time, and nobody ever sees it. What drives her is the seed's line: she cannot save everyone, so she
will save as many as she can.

**Inferred, for the founders:** she carries somebody else's sins. Above, before the fall, she did
something as a commander that Ultor will kill her for. She does not remember it, and she is not that
person now. The seed lived inside a hated tyrant's name; she lives under the shadow of her own
forgotten self.

- **Fight — Command.** Passive: allies near her fill their charge faster. Her moves are orders:
  _advance_ (everyone near her moves), _hold_ (everyone near her takes less damage), _break them_
  (everyone near her hits harder for a few seconds), and one spear thrust of her own. On her own she
  is ordinary. With five people behind her she is the best leader of the sixteen.
- **Party:** the bigger the party, the stronger she is. She is the obvious leader at an S-rank gate,
  and in some worlds she is holding one when you meet her. Placeholder body: `legion_gold`.
- **What she tells you:** what you need to carry out an order. Never how bad the odds are.
- **Earned name:** "the Last Wall", if the fortress holds.
- **Difficulty:** 2.

### Cassius — The Contrary (ENTP)

_Seed: Garou._

One of the four names spoken in the session. `dougie`'s example lead was "you choose Cassius".
Cassius does not believe in heroes. He has seen how a crowd decides who the hero is: whoever is
popular and strong, while someone else is made to play the monster. So he argues for the monster.
Is the thing that came out of the gate evil, or only different? Is the bandit a bandit, or only
poor? He lands in a city and is in its politics within the month, always on the side everyone else
has decided is the villain.

- **Fight — Riposte.** Passive: every hit Cassius takes teaches him the one who dealt it. He answers
  that enemy harder for the rest of the fight. His moves are a taunt that makes one enemy come for
  him, a parry, a counter that hits harder the more he has taken, and a feint. He never swings first
  if he can help it.
- **Party:** fine in one, as long as he is allowed to argue with its leader. **He will not travel
  with Caesar, and Caesar has no idea.** Caesar is the hero every crowd cheers for, and that is
  exactly what Cassius cannot stand. The asymmetry is the Baldur's Gate draft on `CH9`
  ([16.6](16-lineage/16.6-baldurs-gate-3.md)), and the history behind the two names does the rest.
  Placeholder body: `ember_duelist`.
- **What he tells you:** anything, if you can win the argument for it.
- **Earned name:** "the Monster". He was given it as an insult and he answers to it.
- **Difficulty:** 3.

## The other twelve, in brief

### NF

- **Saturn — The Quiet Cause (INFJ), from Pain.** Saturn is the god who was thrown out of heaven,
  and he is the one of the sixteen who goes wrong. **Inferred reason, for `dougie` to replace:** he
  thinks the fall was deserved. The world below should hurt until it understands why, and he means
  to make it. He gathers the desperate into a creed that grows every chapter. _Fight — Gravity:_
  pushes everything away from him, pulls one thing in, and slows the ground around him. He tells
  you nothing until he trusts you, and then he tells you too much. Body: `golden_knight`.
- **Vesta — The Kept Flame (INFP), from Jaehwan.** Every one of the sixteen climbs the Tower to get
  back to where they fell from. **Vesta will not go back.** The world she landed in is the one she
  keeps, and she will not trade it for the one before. She keeps one fire lit where she landed, and
  she keeps it alone if everyone else leaves, as they tend to. Her coldness comes from that
  loneliness. She is not heartless. _Fight — Ward:_ shields and heals, and her fire burns only the
  dead, the past that will not stay down. **Inferred:** as the lead, she is the one character who
  would rather not hear the ghosts of dead leads, and the player chooses whether she listens. She
  tells you only what is true now. Body: `legion_white`.
- **Caesar — The Gatherer (ENFJ), from Rengoku and Ash Ketchum.** "my name is Caesar, but everyone
  calls me a kid" — doug-md [00:08:02]. He joins the Adventurers Guild on the first day and loses his
  first fights. He keeps getting back up, and he hunts goblins until the name sticks: _Goblin Hunter
  Caesar_. He believes the strong exist to protect the weak, and he means it every time he says it.
  Everyone likes him, including Cassius, as far as Caesar knows. _Fight — Shield-bearer:_ takes the
  hits meant for the people beside him. Passive: once a fight, when he falls, he gets back up. He
  tells you everything. Body: `magic_swordsman`.
- **Iris — The Spark (ENFP), from Isagi.** The messenger. She goes between towns carrying other
  people's news, and half the rumours on the job board started with her. That makes her the
  character who ties into the rumour and job systems. She always passes the ball. Somewhere above,
  she once handed someone else the decisive moment and it went wrong, and she does not remember
  that, only the flinch. _Fight — Skirmish:_ the fastest of the sixteen, she marks targets for the
  party. Passive: finishing a marked target herself makes her stronger for the rest of the fight.
  Her story is learning to take the shot. Body: `jester_thief`.

### SJ

- **Pluto — The Ledger (ISTJ), from Sung Jin-Woo and Kang Taeha.** Pluto is lord of the dead and of
  what is buried in the ground. He lands as the weakest of the sixteen, E-rank at the Guild. Unlike
  the others, he _feels_ that he was once at the top. He does not remember it; he is simply certain.
  He is proud, cold, and patient: he has done this before, and he will do it again. He grinds one
  enemy kind at a time, which makes him the character built around first-kill experience
  ([D37](06-decisions.md)). _Fight — Shades:_ raises what he kills to fight for him for the rest of
  the battle, and gets stronger with each kind he has killed. He does not want a party and does not
  need one. Body: `dusk_shadow`.
- **Ceres — The Keeper (ISFJ), from Jin Tae-Kyung.** She lands at the bottom: no rank, no name, in a
  village that is not managing. She stays anyway and keeps it fed. The village becomes her family,
  and she keeps going when she is plainly outmatched. She is cheeky, funny, and completely sincere.
  By the third chapter the village answers to her. She will not leave it until it is safe, which
  makes her the hardest of the sixteen to recruit (`MX18`). _Fight — Growth:_ plants the ground:
  thorns that hold enemies, and grain that heals whoever stands in it. She is strong wherever she
  has been standing for a while. Body: `peasant`.
- **Cato — The Straight Road (ESTJ), from Hansu Lee / Bjorn Yandel.** Cato was the Roman who ended
  every speech the same way. He works at the Guild's bounty office and knows its rules better than
  the people who wrote them, because rules are what keep you alive. He never hopes for the best; he
  prepares for the worst, and he is usually right. Under the dour outside he has noticed what the
  rules do to the people they land on, and it bothers him more than he lets anyone see. He hunts
  bandits, which puts him on a collision course with Ultor. _Fight — Formation:_ stronger when he
  does not move, and stronger again with an ally beside him. He is the wall the others stand behind.
  Body: `legion_black`.
- **Juno — The Full Table (ESFJ), from Sanji.** Juno is the patron of the household. She runs an inn
  that everyone passes through, and nobody at her table goes hungry, enemies included. **Inferred:**
  she was the soft one above, the one the others thought weak for caring. Down here, her kindness is
  a choice she makes every day on purpose. Because everyone passes through, she knows where every one
  of the fifteen was last seen. _Fight — Provision:_ every potion, food and item gives her twice the
  effect, and she can throw them to an ally. She fights with her feet, because her hands are for the
  kitchen. She tells you everything you ask, and remembers that you asked. Body: `devil_butler`.

### SP

- **Ultor — The Steady Hand (ISTP), from Thorfinn and Zoro.** "Ultor" is Mars the Avenger. He is
  dual-wielding, lives among bandits, and is getting stronger for one reason: to kill one person.
  **Inferred, for the founders:** that person is **Bellona**. He does not remember why, and he knows
  her by the mark. She has no idea, and would gladly take him into her army. He answers to nobody,
  until someone earns it. Then he chooses them as his captain, the way the seed chose his, and
  would die for them. _Fight — Twin blades:_ the fastest attacks in the game, with everything aimed
  at one marked target. He fights alone until he has chosen. Body: `storm_blade`.
- **Diana — The Own Path (ISFP), from King.** The huntress. Every village near her forest believes
  she is the most dangerous thing in it. **It is the wolf.** Diana is a quiet, anxious person who
  would rather be left alone. The kills people credit her with are her wolf's, and she has never
  corrected them. She wishes she were the person they are afraid of. _Fight — Beast-bond:_ the wolf
  does the damage; Diana's moves keep it alive and send it in. If the wolf falls, she is nearly
  helpless. **Inferred:** her standing in the places around her forest (`Renown`) starts far above
  what she can back up. She only joins a party if it will have the wolf too. Body: `frozen_wolfman`,
  with `ice_wolf` as the wolf.
- **Ares — The First Move (ESTP), from Garp.** The Greek god of war, and one of the names spoken in
  the session. He is loud, laughing, the strongest of his generation, and the champion of whichever
  kingdom he landed in. He is loyal to it while seeing every one of its faults. When it sends him
  after its outlaws, he goes. **Inferred, for the founders:** one of those outlaws is Liber, who was
  his kin above. Neither remembers, the mark tells them nothing about why, and Ares lets him go
  anyway. _Fight — Opener:_ the heaviest first ten seconds in the game, then he tires. He is the
  easiest of the sixteen to play. Body: `sword_bear`.
- **Liber — The Bright Hour (ESFP), from Luffy.** Liber is the Roman god of freedom. He never stays
  in the same place twice, and if there is a sea, he is on it. _Fight — Bare hands:_ unarmed, and
  he cannot be pinned, rooted or disarmed. He gets more reckless as the fight goes on. His
  signature weapon is a pair of cestus, and he would rather not wear them. Body: `cabin_boy`.

## Sixteen ways to fight

No two of these styles share a passive. All of them fit the real-time fight
([18](18-combat-direction.md#the-prototype)): four moves on Q W E R and one passive.

| How it wins | Styles |
| --- | --- |
| Damage from range | Atlas (borne), Iris (skirmish) |
| Damage up close | Ultor (twin blades), Ares (opener), Liber (bare hands), Cassius (riposte), Diana (beast-bond, through the wolf) |
| Controls the field | Janus (doorways), Saturn (gravity), Ceres (growth) |
| Keeps people up | Vesta (ward), Caesar (shield-bearer), Juno (provision) |
| Makes others better | Bellona (command), Cato (formation), Pluto (shades) |

**Inferred, for [agenda item 10](worldbuilding/answers.md#what-is-still-to-decide):** each of the
sixteen gets a class of their own, carrying their passive and four moves. The six existing classes
stay as the classes anyone can learn. This matches "16 different ways of fighting" and "available to
get different classes". It means sixteen new classes rather than six, and that is the founders' call.

## What the founders need to decide

1. **Keep, change or replace each of the sixteen**, starting with the NT four (Atlas, Janus,
   Bellona, Cassius).
2. **Which seed goes to which character.** The pairings above are a proposal. Any seed can move.
3. **Pain's reason.** `dougie` was cut off mid-thought [00:17:59]. The draft gives Saturn one; the
   real one replaces it.
4. **Who carries Atlas:** someone hired, a friend, one of the other fifteen, or a beast, as in the
   placeholder art.
5. **A class of one's own for each** (agenda item 10), or styles inside the six classes.
6. **The four ties above**: keep them, change them or drop them.
7. **The Taeha seed's title.** The summary it came with named Kang Taeha, reborn as Yu Shin-un, and
   was itself unsure which series was meant. Pluto takes only the trait, "I've already reached the top
   once. I'll do it again.", so nothing here depends on the title. Worth checking all the same.
8. **Cassius as a regional name.** `data/names.json` lists _Cassius_ as an ordinary noble name, and
   its own notes say the sixteen's names are not in it. If Cassius is one of the sixteen, he comes
   out of that list.

## What happens next

Once a character is confirmed, it is built as the six-file bundle in
[13](13-heroes-and-tempers.md#writing-one), on an existing body so nothing waits on art. The
temper quiz starts landing on them as each one is built. Backstory stays late, as the session asked
[00:20:20].
