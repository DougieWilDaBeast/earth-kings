[joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md) for both founders to keep,

- **The carried mage → INTJ.** A man who has already decided how everything ends, and who cannot

## The NT sitting — the first four to build

This is the first sitting on the [roadmap](05-roadmap.md). These four are drafted in the most detail
so they can be built as soon as they are confirmed.

### Atlas — The Long Plan (INTJ)

Named for the Titan who held up the sky. This one cannot hold up himself. He is small and he cannot
walk properly, so someone has to carry him. When he casts, nothing else on the field comes close.
People look down on him, often literally. He is patient with it the way a man is patient with the
weather, because he has already decided how this ends.

- **Fight — Borne.** Passive: Atlas has no move of his own. He moves only when a companion is
  carrying him, and if that companion falls, he is stuck where they dropped him. His four moves are
  the longest-range and slowest-casting in the game. Losing your bearer is how Atlas loses.
- **Party:** he always needs one person, the bearer. He is never alone, and he is never free. Who
  carries him is the character's real question: someone hired, someone loyal, or someone who owes
  him. Placeholder body: `lizard_wizard` ("Tiny blind wizard riding a lizard"), who is already being
  carried.
- **What he tells you:** exactly what you need for the next step of his plan, and nothing more.
- **Earned name:** people who dislike him call him "the Baggage".
- **Difficulty:** 4.

### Janus — The Open Question (INTP)

The god of doorways and of beginnings and endings. After landing, the first thing Janus does is walk
into a gate to see how it works. Everyone else sees a threat. Janus sees a question nobody has
thought to ask properly.

- **Fight — Doorways.** Passive: every so often, a unit Janus hits is moved a tile. His moves swap
  two units, pull an ally out of trouble, and send an enemy into a bad spot. He does very little
  damage himself; he wins fights by putting everyone where he wants them.
- **Party:** easy company, and prone to wandering off to look at something. Placeholder body:
  `timeless_one` ("The all knowing man who stands at the end of time").
- **What he tells you:** everything, including the parts that are only guesses, without saying
  which parts those are.
- **Earned name:** "the Gatewalker". The Guild calls him that because he keeps coming back out.
- **Difficulty:** 3.

### Bellona — The Marshal (ENTJ)

The Roman goddess of war, and one of the four names spoken in the session. Wherever Bellona lands,
there is an army within a week, and within a chapter she is commanding it. Kingdoms are glad of her
until they notice who the soldiers answer to now.

- **Fight — Command.** Passive: allies near her fill their charge faster. Her moves are orders:
  _advance_ (everyone near her moves), _hold_ (everyone near her takes less damage), _break them_
  (everyone near her hits harder for a few seconds), and one spear thrust of her own. On her own she
  is ordinary. With five people behind her she is the best leader of the sixteen.
- **Party:** the bigger the party, the stronger she is. She is the obvious leader at an S-rank gate.
  Placeholder body: `legion_gold`.
- **What she tells you:** what you need to carry out an order.
- **Earned name:** "the Standard".
- **Difficulty:** 2.

### Cassius — The Contrary (ENTP)

One of the four names spoken in the session. `dougie`'s example lead was "you choose Cassius". He
argues because it is the fastest way to find the weak point of an idea or a man, and he usually
finds it. He lands in a city and is in its politics within the month, always on the side that is
losing, because that side is more interesting.

- **Fight — Riposte.** Passive: every hit Cassius takes charges his answer. His moves are a taunt
  that makes one enemy come for him, a parry, a counter that hits harder the more he has taken, and
  a feint. He never swings first if he can help it.
- **Party:** fine in one, as long as he is allowed to argue with its leader. **He will not travel
  with Caesar, and Caesar has no idea.** That asymmetry is the Baldur's Gate draft on `CH9`
  ([16.6](16-lineage/16.6-baldurs-gate-3.md)), and the history behind the two names does the rest.
  Placeholder body: `ember_duelist`.
- **What he tells you:** anything, if you can win the argument for it.
- **Earned name:** "the Lean One", from the look people remember about him.
- **Difficulty:** 3.

## The other twelve, in brief

### NF

- **Saturn — The Quiet Cause (INFJ), from Pain.** Saturn is the god who was thrown out of heaven,
  and he is the one of the sixteen who goes wrong. **Inferred reason, for `dougie` to replace:** he
  thinks the fall was deserved. The world below should hurt until it understands why, and he means
  to make it. He gathers the desperate into a creed that grows every chapter. _Fight — Gravity:_
  pushes everything away from him, pulls one thing in, and slows the ground around him. He tells
  you nothing until he trusts you, and then he tells you too much. Body: `golden_knight`.
- **Vesta — The Kept Flame (INFP).** The keeper of the hearth fire. She will not kill a living
  thing, even when it costs the company (`CH5`). _Fight — Ward:_ shields and heals. Her fire burns
  only the dead and the things that come out of gates, and living enemies are left on the ground
  rather than killed. Body: `legion_white`.
- **Caesar — The Gatherer (ENFJ).** "my name is Caesar, but everyone calls me a kid" — doug-md
  [00:08:02]. He joins the Adventurers Guild on the first day and hunts goblins until the name
  sticks: _Goblin Hunter Caesar_. Everyone likes him, including Cassius, as far as Caesar knows.
  _Fight — Shield-bearer:_ takes the hits meant for the people beside him, and gets stronger the
  more of the party are still standing. Body: `magic_swordsman`.
- **Iris — The Spark (ENFP).** The messenger. She goes between towns carrying news, and half the
  rumours on the job board started with her. That makes her the character who ties into the
  rumour and job systems. _Fight — Skirmish:_ the fastest of the sixteen, marks a target for the
  party, and is gone before it turns round. Body: `jester_thief`.

### SJ

- **Pluto — The Ledger (ISTJ), from Sung Jin-Woo.** Pluto is lord of the dead and of what is buried
  in the ground. He lands as the weakest of the sixteen, E-rank at the Guild, and grinds, one enemy
  kind at a time. That makes him the character built around first-kill experience
  ([D37](06-decisions.md)). _Fight — Shades:_ raises what he kills to fight for him for the rest of
  the battle, and gets stronger with each kind he has killed. He does not want a party and does not
  need one. Body: `dusk_shadow`.
- **Ceres — The Keeper (ISFJ).** Wherever she lands, she stays, and makes it a village that eats.
  She will not leave it until it is safe, which makes her the hardest of the sixteen to recruit
  (`MX18`). _Fight — Growth:_ plants the ground: thorns that hold enemies, and grain that heals
  whoever stands in it. She is strong wherever she has been standing for a while. Body: `peasant`.
- **Cato — The Straight Road (ESTJ).** Cato was the Roman who ended every speech the same way. He
  brings the law wherever he lands: a town watch, or the Guild's bounty office, hunting bandits.
  That puts him on a collision course with Ultor. _Fight — Formation:_ stronger when he does not
  move, and stronger again with an ally beside him. Body: `legion_black`.
- **Juno — The Full Table (ESFJ).** Juno is the patron of the household. She runs an inn that
  everyone passes through, and because of that she knows where every one of the fifteen was last
  seen. _Fight — Provision:_ every potion, food and item gives her twice the effect, and she can
  throw them to an ally. She tells you everything you ask, and remembers that you asked. Body:
  `devil_butler`.

### SP

- **Ultor — The Steady Hand (ISTP), from Thorfinn.** "Ultor" is Mars the Avenger. He is
  dual-wielding and bandit-like, and he is getting stronger for one reason: to kill one person.
  **Inferred, for the founders:** that person is **Bellona**. He does not remember why, and he knows
  her by the mark. She has no idea, and would gladly take him into her army. _Fight — Twin blades:_
  the fastest attacks in the game, with everything aimed at one marked target. He fights alone.
  Body: `storm_blade`.
- **Diana — The Own Path (ISFP).** The huntress. She lives in the wild with a pack, and people do
  not see her unless she wants them to. _Fight — Beast-bond:_ fights as two, herself and a wolf she
  commands. If the wolf falls, she fights like half of herself. She only joins a party if it will
  have the wolf too. Body: `frozen_wolfman`, with `ice_wolf` as the wolf.
- **Ares — The First Move (ESTP).** The Greek god of war, and one of the names spoken in the
  session. He goes wherever the fight is: the coliseum, or the gate that opened this morning.
  _Fight — Opener:_ the heaviest first ten seconds in the game, then he tires. He is the easiest of
  the sixteen to play. Body: `sword_bear`.
- **Liber — The Bright Hour (ESFP), from Luffy.** Liber is the Roman god of freedom. He never stays
  in the same place twice, and if there is a sea, he is on it. _Fight — Bare hands:_ unarmed, and
  he cannot be pinned, rooted or disarmed. He gets more reckless as the fight goes on. His
  signature weapon is a pair of cestus, and he would rather not wear them. Body: `cabin_boy`.

## Sixteen ways to fight

No two of these styles share a passive. All of them fit the real-time fight
([18](18-combat-direction.md#the-prototype)): four moves on Q W E R and one passive.

| How it wins | Styles |
| --- | --- |
| Damage from range | Atlas (borne), Iris (skirmish), Diana (beast-bond) |
| Damage up close | Ultor (twin blades), Ares (opener), Liber (bare hands), Cassius (riposte) |
| Controls the field | Janus (doorways), Saturn (gravity), Ceres (growth) |
| Keeps people up | Vesta (ward), Caesar (shield-bearer), Juno (provision) |
| Makes others better | Bellona (command), Cato (formation), Pluto (shades) |

**Inferred, for [agenda item 10](worldbuilding/answers.md#what-is-still-to-decide):** each of the
sixteen gets a class of their own, carrying their passive and four moves. The six existing classes
stay as the classes anyone can learn. This matches "16 different ways of fighting" and "available to
get different classes". It means sixteen new classes rather than six, and that is the founders' call.
