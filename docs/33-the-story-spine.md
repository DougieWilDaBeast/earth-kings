# 33 — The story spine: floors 1 to 100

Written 2026-10-24 for `dougie`'s question of that afternoon: "how do we arrange the story alongside the 100
floors? As we're climbing up it, how do we keep things moving along?" ([D445](06-decisions.md)). It lays every
story beat the game has, or has settled, against the climb, and marks each one:

- **Built**: in the game today.
- **Drafted**: written down, not in the game.
- **Settled**: a founder's rule exists, nothing is built.
- **Blank**: nobody has said anything yet.

Nothing here is built, and no code starts before `doug-md`'s play-test 9 ([D383](06-decisions.md)). The acts and
every **Proposed** beat are the writers' and designers' reading, **Inferred.**, on the agenda (rows 349 to 354) and
asked as call CL, questions 67 to 73 in [30](30-questions-for-the-founders.md).

## The shape in one paragraph

The climb is twenty chapters of five floors ([D48](06-decisions.md)), and each chapter's close moves the world on
and seals the stair until the company answers the world ([D34](06-decisions.md)). That gives the story two tracks
that take turns: **the Tower track** (floors, keepers, the lead's own memories, which only go up) and **the world
track** (gates, companions, the nemesis, crossings, the fallen, which go sideways). Floors 1 to 10 have both
tracks, fully. From floor 11 the world track still runs, but the Tower track is almost empty: the floors are
generated and fought, and no memory, keeper or story person waits on any floor between 11 and 99 except as a
settled rule (floor 30, floor 70) with nothing written. **That gap, floors 11 to 99, is the work.**

## Five acts (proposed)

**Inferred** (agenda 349, question 67). Four chapters to an act, twenty floors each, each act with one turn the
player will remember and one thing it hands the next act. The acts are a writing tool, not a screen: the player
only sees chapters.

| Act | Floors | Chapters | What it is about | Its turn |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I. The Fall | 1 to 20 | 1 to 4 | Who am I, where is this, the first gate, the first company | Floor 10: the ties are named; floor 20: the lead learns there were sixteen above (chapter four's memory, proposed) |
| II. The Climbers | 21 to 40 | 5 to 8 | Other people in the Tower; the world takes notice; the first fallen world reached | Floor 30: a gate opens into a burned world ([D172](06-decisions.md)) |
| III. The Seams | 41 to 60 | 9 to 12 | The worlds leak: dreams, crossings, demon kings who know your face | Floor 50: the faces come back ([CH10](15-the-question-book.md), proposed) |
| IV. The Order | 61 to 80 | 13 to 16 | The legend is real; what the climb is for | Floor 70: the Forgotten Order ([D260](06-decisions.md), [D291](06-decisions.md)) |
| V. The Top | 81 to 100 | 17 to 20 | Only one world can be left; who arranged it | Floor 100: the apex, the contest ([D171](06-decisions.md)), then the merge ([D440](06-decisions.md)) |

After floor 100 comes the merge, the split and the last act ([the ending frame](worldbuilding/the-ending-frame.md),
[D440](06-decisions.md) to [D444](06-decisions.md)), settled in direction and designed in M17.
