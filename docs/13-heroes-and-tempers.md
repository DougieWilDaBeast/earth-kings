# 13 — Heroes & Tempers

Sixteen starting characters, one per temper. The player does not browse them: they answer the
questions, and the answers name the life they are about to live. The other fifteen are people
in the world.

Supersedes [11 — Hero Backstories](11-character-backstories.md), which describes the twelve heroes
this replaces. Since 2026-10-13 the sixteen are the leads ([D78](06-decisions.md)); the twelve are
story NPCs, no longer offered as a lead.

## Who the sixteen are

Settled in [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md) and written up
in [17 — The Fall and the Sixteen Worlds](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md):

- **Fallen demigods** ([D31](06-decisions.md)). All sixteen lived above, at godhood, and fell to
  Earth in the same disaster. They lost their memories, landed at random, and share **one mark**,
  in the same place on each of them, by which they recognise one another.
- **Fixed god-style names**, Roman and Greek, the same every run ([D39](06-decisions.md)). Spoken as
  examples, not yet assigned: Cassius, Caesar, Bellona, Ares.
- **Sixteen ways to fight.** "you kind of want 16 different characters that have to have 16
  different ways of fighting" — each following their temper. Six classes cannot give sixteen
  styles; [agenda item 10](worldbuilding/answers.md#what-is-still-to-decide).
- **Their own lives.** When not the lead, each lives by their temper from wherever they landed,
  with their own line of quests to the Tower. **Built 2026-10-12** without the quest lines: they
  crash-land at seeded sites and walk between kinds of place by temper, and can be met and joined
  ([17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md#the-others-as-built)). Since 2026-10-13 they are the sixteen
  themselves, each settling first at the kind of place [21](21-the-sixteen-first-draft.md) puts them
  ([D78](06-decisions.md)). The twelve who stood in before are
  story people met in the world ([D79](06-decisions.md)).
- **A signature weapon each**, made for them, god-tier ([D33](06-decisions.md)). It is what
  crosses to the next world when they die as the lead.
- **Backstory is not the priority.** "I'm not too worried about what someone's backstory is or what
  their ambitions are. That's part of the game" — `dougie`, [00:20:20]. The pools in
  [14](14-lore-pools.md) still supply it, late.

## The questions

Four questions, one per preference pair, two answers each: sixteen combinations of answers, and
each names a different lead ([D110](06-decisions.md)). The questions live in `data/tempers.json`
under `quiz` and are asked by `src/ui/temper_quiz.gd` ([D80](06-decisions.md)). The code can still
settle a pair by majority if more than one question is written for it, but D110 keeps it to one.

**Wanted from `dougie`: the founders' four questions.** `doug-md` asked on 2026-10-13 for the quiz to
use the questions the founders came up with for choosing a character by personality and choices.
They are not written down anywhere in the repo yet, so the game still asks the four drafts below,
one per pair. What is needed, one per pair:

- **E / I** — where the thinking happens: with others, or alone
- **S / N** — what gets trusted: what is in front of you, or the shape of the thing
- **T / F** — how it gets decided: by weight, or by who it lands on
- **J / P** — how the road gets walked: decide early and hold, or decide late and keep moving

Each one a moment in this world with two answers, one for each side, and no right answer. Send them
in any form; dropping them into `quiz` is a data change and nothing else needs to move. The test the
letters come from is never named in what the player reads.

| Axis | The question is about | Letters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E / I | Where the thinking happens | **Outward** — counts on the room · **Inward** — counts on themselves |
| S / N | What gets trusted | **Ground-read** — the track, the weather, the wound · **Sky-read** — the shape of the thing |
| T / F | How it gets decided | **Cold-eyed** — by weight · **Warm-handed** — by who it lands on |
| J / P | How the road gets walked | **Set** — decides early and holds · **Loose** — decides late and keeps moving |

The in-world name leads everywhere it is shown. The four-letter code appears as a subtitle and as
the key in `data/tempers.json`; **the test it comes from is never named in shipped text.**

## What a temper is worth

Every character carries exactly four leans, one per letter, so nobody is strictly stronger than
anybody else — the sixteen are sixteen mixes of one budget. Values live in `tempers.json` under
`leans` and are meant to be argued with.

| Letter | What it does | Where it happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **E** | Bonds start a step warmer; hires cost 15% less | `Banter.initial_bond`, `Market.asking_hire_cost` |
| **I** | Doctrine fades at 1125 steps instead of 900 | `Doctrine.fade_after` |
| **S** | Proficiency — with a move and with a weapon — arrives 25% sooner | `Proficiency.rank`, `Proficiency.arms_rank` |
| **N** | Reads generated trees as if the Codex were 25% further along | `Progression` |
| **T** | +5% damage dealt | `Character.attack` |
| **F** | Rescue grace +4pp per ally standing, cap +8pp | `Fate.graces_for` |
| **J** | Opens a fight acting at once — its first look and swing come before anyone else's (was +20 charge time in the turn-based fight) | `Fighter._init` (`opening_ct`) |
| **P** | +1 move point | `Character.move_points` |

## The screen

`New Game` with nobody fallen is Atlas's last stand ([D191](06-decisions.md), replacing
[D106](06-decisions.md)'s first life as Atlas): he falls in Hearthwick, and the way on from his fall,
like every New Game after it, goes to `temper_quiz`, not to the picker. The quiz is four questions, one per pair, two answers each: sixteen ways
through, each naming a different lead ([D110](06-decisions.md)). The quiz asks its questions over the falling sky
of the start screen, four marks at the top lighting as each pair is settled, then reveals
the character: portrait, name, title, temper and code, warband, whatever authored history has been
cast on them, and their stats.

**A temper with nobody written for it is an ordinary state, not a bug.** The sixteen slots exist in
`tempers.json` from the start and fill in one at a time; an unwritten slot says so and offers the
full roster instead.

**The quiz is the front door** ([D44](06-decisions.md)). Nothing else is offered until the
questions are answered. Then the reveal offers two side doors next to _This is you_:
**Answer again**, which goes back to the first question as often as the player likes, and
**Show me all sixteen**, the old picker, which is also how the whole roster gets tested.

**The dead cannot be answered into** ([D43](06-decisions.md)). The quiz takes the temper codes of
fallen leads as `boot_payload.fallen` and leaves out any answer whose every temper has fallen. A
pair left with one answer is still asked once, and the player has to pick it: seeing the gap is the
point. The rest of that pair's questions are skipped. Since 2026-10-01 the dead are passed in ([D47](06-decisions.md)): a lead who dies is
written to `user://earth-kings.sixteen.json` (`src/chronicle/sixteen.gd`), apart from the save and
the museum. New Game hands their tempers to the quiz and their heroes on to _Show me all sixteen_,
which offers only the living. Once nobody is left, the picker offers **Begin a new sixteen**. It asks
twice, then wipes the record.

## Writing one

Four sittings of four, grouped so each sitting contrasts with itself: **NT** (INTJ, INTP, ENTJ,
ENTP), **NF**, **SJ**, **SP**. Each character ships as one bundle:

- `data/heroes.json` — id, `title`, `temper`, `difficulty`, `origin`, `blurb`, `companions`,
  and for when somebody else is the lead: `found_at`, `elsewhere`, `first`, `rumour` (see
  [04](04-data-formats.md#heroesjson--the-leads-a-run-can-be-started-as))
- `data/units.json` — stats, one basic attack in `abilities`, `color`, `sprite_dir` (`art/sixteen/<id>/idle`);
  no `classes`, so the level-2 switch offers every class
- `data/classes.json` — their own class, with `lead` set to their id
- `data/fall.json` → `wanderers` — nothing to add once `types["XXXX"].hero` is set: the temper
  there decides where they walk when somebody else is the lead, beside their own `found_at`, and the
  lines are their temper group's except where `first` and `rumour` give them their own
- `data/dialogue/heroes/<id>.json` — what they say, including what it takes to join
- this document — an entry in the shape `docs/11` uses
- `data/tempers.json` — `types["XXXX"].hero`

Two rules:

1. **Show the type, never state it.** The character reads as their temper through what they do and
   refuse to do. The four letters are a subtitle, not a description.
2. **Art before identity.** Twenty-eight unit sprite directories already exist. Give each new
   character an existing body and nothing waits on art.

Their five history fields — background, grudge, hearth, creed, oath — stay blank. Those are
written separately and cast late; see [14 — Lore Pools](14-lore-pools.md). A character has to read
as themselves before any of it is attached.

## The lines

Every line said in the game had one writers' pass on 2026-10-19 ([D274](06-decisions.md), `doug-md`'s
play-test 7, item 14): about 430 of some 3,850 lines in `data/` rewritten, the rest left as they were.
A line written since keeps to the same rules:

- **Its speaker's own voice.** Thirty folk of Stormreach are thirty people, not one wry narrator; a fen is
  patient and damp, a shore wreck-wary. Ser Valis speaks without contractions; Atlas too. The seven
  companies climbing the Tower are seven voices too ([D341](06-decisions.md), play-test 8): the Copper Hands
  count coin, the Lantern Company draws maps, the Ninth Stair says little, Hob's Lot says a lot, the Grey
  Oath speaks without contractions, the Saltmen talk like the boats they left, the Late Risers are late.
- **Plain and spare.** British English, short sentences, concrete things over abstractions; the Tower
  takes a capital.
- **No stock tics.** No em-dash in a line; no "its own kind of", "a new kind of", "the part nobody",
  "and somehow", "something about the way"; no quip tacked on a sad line, no closing aphorism on every line.
- **Not a template.** The memories keep their refrain ("the floor goes out from under the world", "White.",
  the journal page), but each closes in its own way.
- **Prices said are prices paid.** A line that names a cost names the one the option charges (100 copper is
  a silver, [26](26-coins-and-prices.md)).

`python3 tools/lines.py` lists any tic left in `data/` (none since the pass); `python3 tools/lines.py --script
out.md` writes every line, file by file, as one script to read through. Cato's chapter one (the draft `dougie`
kept) was not touched. `tools/chapter_twos.py` writes the chapter twos from the shared drafts, so running it
again undoes the pass on them unless the drafts are brought in line first.
