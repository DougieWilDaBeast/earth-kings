# The climb's arcs, act by act

Written 2026-10-24 from `dougie`'s word that afternoon: take the open blanks, turn them into arcs, break them into
sections "that me and you can understand", and see "what's available with the characters we have and the storylines
that they would end up creating" ([D446](../06-decisions.md)). It builds on [33 — the story spine](../33-the-story-spine.md)
([D445](../06-decisions.md)) and goes ahead on its recommendations while call CL is out: five acts, the memory cadence
(full scenes at chapters 3, 4, 6, 10, 14 and 20, a line at every other), the faces at floor 50, the Order met at 70, a
keeper of the Tower's own at 100. If `dougie` answers CL otherwise, this changes with it.

The storyline each of the sixteen makes across these acts is its own sheet: [the sixteen's storylines](the-sixteen-storylines.md).

**Direction, not the build.** Nothing here is in the game, and no code starts before `doug-md`'s play-test 9
([D383](../06-decisions.md)). Every proposed beat is **Inferred.** (agenda 358 to 361) and goes back to the founders.

## How to read it

Every act has the same five parts:

1. **What the act is for**: one sentence, what the player should feel by its end.
2. **The arcs**: each a short chain of beats, with the floors it runs on, who carries it, and its state.
3. **What it hands the next act.**
4. **What blocks it**: the open question, and who answers it.

States: **Built** (in the game), **Drafted** (written, not in), **Settled** (a founder's rule, nothing written),
**Proposed** (ours, Inferred), **Blank** (nobody has said). Who answers: **Will** (`dougie`), **Ewan** (`doug-md`),
**Both**, or **us** (the writers and designers drafting for the founders to change).

Two rules hold in every act:

- **The Tower track goes up, the world track goes sideways.** A chapter's close seals the stair until the company does
  something in the world ([D34](../06-decisions.md)), so every act needs at least one reason out into the world per
  chapter, and one reason back up.
- **Every fall changes the next act.** A lead who falls becomes a demon king in later worlds' gates
  ([D304](../06-decisions.md)), a ghost at the fire, a weapon in a crater, and at the merge a demon king who knows
  everything ([D440](../06-decisions.md)). An arc that names one of the sixteen says what happens if they have fallen.
