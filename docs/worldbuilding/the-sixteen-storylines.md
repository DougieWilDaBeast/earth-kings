# The sixteen's storylines, chapter one to the merge

Written 2026-10-24 beside [the climb's arcs](the-climb-arcs.md) ([D446](../06-decisions.md)): `dougie` asked to see "what's
available with the characters we have and the storylines that they would end up creating". One sheet per lead, the
same rows each, from what exists (chapter one, chapter two, the companion story) through what we propose for the
memories the climb still lacks (the cadence of call CL: full scenes at chapters 3, 4, 6, 10, 14 and 20) to the paired
goals and the merge ([the ending frame](the-ending-frame.md)).

**Direction, not the build.** Chapters one and two and the companion stories are in the game; every other row is
**Inferred.** (agenda 359), drawn from [21](../21-the-sixteen-first-draft.md), the chapter-two drafts' "where it could go"
(the project's `story/chapter-two/`), Will's three volumes and the paired goals. Each sheet ends with its gaps and who
fills them. Nothing is built before play-test 9 ([D383](../06-decisions.md)).

**Drafted since** ([D447](../06-decisions.md)): rows 3 and 4 are written as full scenes for all sixteen, in the chapter ones'
and twos' shape, kept in the project's shared files (`story/chapter-three/`, `story/chapter-four/`) and not in this
repository until floors 15 and 20 have hand-laid alcoves to hold them, as the chapter twos were ([D182](../06-decisions.md)).
Where a draft differs from a row below, the draft is newer.

## What the sixteen make together

- **Four ties carry the climb.** Bellona and Ultor, Cassius and Caesar, Ares and Liber, Cato and Ultor: named at 10,
  met again at 15, faces at 50, last meeting before 100, then the paired goals turn each tie into a fight across the
  sides. Ceres and Juno is a fifth, softer one (both feed strangers), first named in the paired goals.
- **Three keep places for strangers.** Ceres, Juno and Vesta are where the people of burned worlds go in Act III.
- **Two carry the seams.** Janus (the doors) and Iris (the word) are how the Pangaea hints reach the player.
- **Two know the top.** Atlas (read it) and Pluto (won it once) carry the contest's foreshadowing.
- **One goes wrong.** Saturn's creed is the climb's slow villain among the living, one step a chapter.
- **Every fall is felt by someone named.** No lead's sheet has an empty "If they fall" row.
