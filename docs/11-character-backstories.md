# 11 — Hero Backstories & Origins

The twelve playable company founders of _Earth Kings_. Each profile details their lineage, the events that set them onto the road, their personality and ethos, their companions, and their distinct mechanical role in the continent.

---

## 1. Bram — The Sworn Blade

- **Calling / Template:** `sworn_blade`
- **Rating:** Steady (Difficulty 1)
- **Background:** Apprentice Smith (`apprentice_smith`) | **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Raiders & Marauders (`brigand`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Ancestral Village / Hearth Forge
- **Company:** Sera (The Longbow), Toln (The Hedge Priest)

### Backstory

Bram was born in the soot of Oakhaven, a border settlement huddled against the foothills of the Dragonspine Ridge. As a boy, he worked the bellows for Master Kaelen, an aging armorer whose work was stamped into the breastplates of three duchies. Bram was taught that steel remembers the strike that forged it, and that an oath sworn over an anvil cannot be filed away.

When the Iron Hand raiders rode out of the broken hills, the guard militia shattered within an hour. Master Kaelen shoved Bram into the cellar beneath the quench tank with an armful of heavy blades. By the time the flames died and Bram forced the scorched trapdoor open, the anvil was cracked in half and Kaelen lay dead on the threshold. Bram gathered the master’s greatsword, scarred his own forearm with cold iron to seal his vow, and took the open road.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Bram is taciturn, patient, and unshakeable. He views every contract as a debt of blood and steel. He shields Sera and Toln instinctively, taking the frontline blows so they can breathe. He harbours a deep, bitter hatred for organized marauders who prey upon defenseless hearths.

## 2. Sera — The Longbow

- **Calling / Template:** `longbow`
- **Rating:** Wary (Difficulty 2)
- **Background:** Wilderness Stray (`wilderness_stray`) | **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Prowling Beasts (`beast`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Forest Hedge Hut
- **Company:** Toln (The Hedge Priest)

### Backstory

Sera never knew a town wall that did not feel like a cage. Abandoned as an infant in the Whispering Wildwood, she was taken in by a reclusive band of gamekeepers and trappers who lived on game and wild honey. Before she had learned her letters, she knew how to read the bend of fern stems, the scent of crushed pine needles, and the distinct whistling click of prowling wolves.

When a feral blight drove predatory packs out of the marsh fringe, her foster family’s cabin was torn open in the dark. Sera survived only by taking to the high canopy of an ancient oak, picking off beasts with broadheads until her quiver was spent and her bowstring soaked in frost. She came down from the trees into a silent clearing, packed salt and dried venison, and followed the river down toward human roads.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Sharp-tongued, observant, and restless, Sera treats stone streets with suspicion and dense brush as home. She banters constantly with Toln to mask the tension of being exposed in the open. In battle, she dictates terms from thirty paces, punishing any creature that attempts to flank the company.

## 3. Toln — The Hedge Priest

- **Calling / Template:** `hedge_priest`
- **Rating:** Hard (Difficulty 3)
- **Background:** Cloistered Scholar (`cloistered_scholar`) | **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Ancestral Grudge:** The Dusk & Abominations (`the_dusk`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Monastery Library / Cloister
- **Company:** Bram (The Sworn Blade)

### Backstory

Toln grew up in the scriptorium of Saint Merryn, secluded high among the chalk hills. For thirty years, his world consisted of the scrape of quills on sheepskin, herbal tisanes brewed from dried meadow grass, and the quiet rhythmic chanting of the morning canonical hours. He transcribed forgotten medical treatises and preserved old doctrine on the fading of souls.

The monastery’s peace ended when an abyssal gate opened in the gorge below. The monks believed their prayers would hold the archway closed; Toln was the only one who noticed that the incense was turning black and the chalk spring was running red. When the dusk horrors crawled up the cliff paths, Toln dragged Bram—then a half-dead wandering blade who had collapsed on the chapel steps—into the catacombs and tended his mortal wounds. Together, they cut their way through the breached sanctuary.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Gentle, weary, yet morally resolute, Toln carries a weathered birch mending staff and the weight of dead brothers. He knows that without Bram's iron, he would have died ten miles back; he heals with quiet focus, reminding the company that blood washed from steel does not wash from memory.

## 4. The Fox Fire Knight

- **Calling / Template:** `fox_knight`
- **Rating:** Punishing (Difficulty 4)
- **Background:** Exiled Noble (`exiled_noble`) | **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Imperial Usurpers (`heart_empire`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Ancestral Keep Yard
- **Company:** Rides alone

### Backstory

He was Lord Jesse of House Renais, third scion of the autumn courts that once held the southern marches. When imperial agents poisoned the duke’s wells and forged a treasonous treaty, Jesse’s elder brothers were executed in the fortress courtyard and his family banner was torn down. Jesse was spared solely because he was away on knightly vigil, returning to find imperial banners flying from the keep’s towers.

Rather than bending the knee or hiring mercenary sellswords to stage a suicidal charge, Jesse adopted the sigil of the Fox Fire—an elusive woodland spirit that ignites brush to lure hunters into peat bogs. Cloaked in tarnished mail and carrying a scimitar infused with searing embers, he vanished into the hinterlands.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Jesse is austere, fiercely disciplined, and solitary. He rejects companions because he refuses to write more names on his conscience. He fights with blistering speed and firebrand strikes, striking before enemies can consolidate their lines. His blade burns with cold aristocratic vengeance.

## 5. The Village Idiot

- **Calling / Template:** `village_idiot`
- **Rating:** Ruinous (Difficulty 5)
- **Background:** Outcast Drifter (`outcast_drifter`) | **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Goblins & Vermin (`goblin`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Broken Frontier Gate
- **Company:** Walks alone

### Backstory

No two villages agree on where Barnaby came from. In Highbarrow he was chased out for untying the butcher’s swine during a wedding; in Fen-on-the-Hill he was thrown in the duck pond after sleeping in the chapel’s sacred bread trough. Barnaby lacks fear, cunning, and common sense. When thunderstorms crack across the plain, he climbs the nearest hay rick to sing doggerel at the clouds.

Yet Barnaby possesses an unnatural, oblivious constitution. Goblins have ambushed him three times in roadside ditches; each time, Barnaby believed they were playing a game of tag, tripped the war-chief into a briar patch, and wandered off with their camp kettle. He marches along the highways humming tuneless ditties, completely unaware that he is traversing the most lethal continent in recorded memory.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Absurd, resilient, and utterly unpredictable. Barnaby has no grand quest and remembers nothing longer than three miles back. His survival is a living insult to the grim mathematics of the world—he swings wildly, blunders through ambushes, and survives blows that would cleave a trained legionnaire in two.

## 6. Dirte — The God of Kings

- **Calling / Template:** `dirte`
- **Rating:** Steady (Difficulty 1)
- **Background:** Exiled Noble (`exiled_noble`) | **Alignment:** Lawful Good
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Imperial Usurpers (`heart_empire`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Sacked Imperial Keep
- **Company:** Legion Silver, Legion Copper

### Backstory

General Titus Dirte was once the living shield of the Heart Empire. In thirty campaigns across forty rivers, Dirte had never yielded a palisade or surrendered a banner. His soldiers worshipped him; the common folk composed ballads about his gilded breastplate. That adoration proved his undoing. Fearing his renown, the corrupt inner council staged a palace coup, declaring Dirte a traitor and ordering his execution.

When the imperial praetorians marched on his garrison, Dirte’s soldiers refused to lay down their spears. Rather than drown the capital in civil slaughter, Dirte took two veteran legionnaires—Centurion Silver and Decurion Copper—and marched out through the iron portcullis into exile.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Regal, sorrowful, and towering in stature. Dirte speaks with the quiet cadence of a ruler who has seen cities rise and burn. He is flanked by two unyielding legionnaires who move in lockstep formation. On the battlefield, Dirte is an immovable fortress; on the road, few villagers dare look him in the eye, knowing he could buy their town or raze it before breakfast.

## 7. Staff Panda — The Bamboo Master

- **Calling / Template:** `staff_panda`
- **Rating:** Wary (Difficulty 2)
- **Background:** Cloistered Scholar (`cloistered_scholar`) | **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
- **Ancestral Grudge:** The Dusk & Abominations (`the_dusk`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Mountain Monastery Cloister
- **Company:** Red Panda Master

### Backstory

High on the Mist-Veiled Crags of the Eastern Ridge stands the Monastery of the Stalk. For sixty years, Master Bo studied the five harmonies of wind and bamboo, mastering the art of redirection and non-lethal martial restraint. He took the Sacred Vow of Wood: never to draw edged steel against flesh, and never to spill blood where patience could break a resolve.

Accompanied by his junior disciple and companion, Master Xiao (the Red Panda), Bo descended from the clouds when unnatural tremors cracked the lower temple steps. The ancient seals below were bleeding abyssal mist, and the balance of the nine winds had tipped toward chaos.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Bo is serene, humorous, and deceptively deadly. He walks with a plain bamboo pole, speaking in gentle proverbs while Master Xiao grumbles about dinner. In combat, Bo’s staff cracks armor with terrifying velocity, redirecting kinetic force and shattering enemy guards without ever unsheathing a blade.

## 8. The Three-Eyed Smith

- **Calling / Template:** `dwarf_smith`
- **Rating:** Wary (Difficulty 2)
- **Background:** Apprentice Smith (`apprentice_smith`) | **Alignment:** Neutral Good
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Raiders & Marauders (`brigand`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Subterranean Hearth Village
- **Company:** Peasant, Village Idiot

### Backstory

Buri Deepforge was the chief metallurgist of the Iron Cairn halls. His third eye—a glowing subterranean opal graft set into his brow by the guild elders—allows him to perceive stress fractures, heat gradients, and impurities in both mined ore and forged armaments. He spent his life forging pickaxes and fortress hinges, until subterranean chasm horrors surged into the lower mine shafts.

Buri evacuated seventy common miners and surface peasants before triggering the tunnel cave-in with his own war sledge. Left on the surface with nothing but his apron, his anvil hammer, and two bewildered surface dwellers who clung to his wake, Buri turned his gaze toward the Spire.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Blunt, practical, and protective of the simple folk trailing behind him. Buri views every enemy as an assemblage of rivets and hinges that simply need to be struck at the proper angle. He treats the Peasant and the Idiot like clumsy nephews, keeping between them and incoming claws.

## 9. The Eightfold Monk

- **Calling / Template:** `element_monk`
- **Rating:** Hard (Difficulty 3)
- **Background:** Cloistered Scholar (`cloistered_scholar`) | **Alignment:** True Neutral
- **Ancestral Grudge:** The Dusk & Abominations (`the_dusk`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Mountain Library / Hermitage
- **Company:** Avatar Adept

### Backstory

Oran was chosen at age seven by the Hermits of the Prism to be the vessel for the Eight Currents: stone, gale, ember, tide, lightning, frost, void, and dawn. In the high caves of the Frostpeak Waste, he spent years sitting naked upon freezing stone slabs, training his spirit to channel pure natural thermodynamics through his palms.

He is accompanied by an Adept whose soul acts as an elemental conduit. When the continental step clock began to accelerate and abnormal seasons broke across the land—snow falling in summer, dried peat catching fire in winter—Oran broke his contemplative silence. The elements were weeping, and the sources of the distortion had to be silenced.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Detached, philosophical, and indifferent to petty human politics. Oran sees life and death as phase transitions of matter. Unarmored and fragile, he maneuvers across the grid with uncanny grace, unleashing devastating elemental bursts that scorch earth and freeze advance corridors.

## 10. The Tidewalker

- **Calling / Template:** `sea_man`
- **Rating:** Hard (Difficulty 3)
- **Background:** Wilderness Stray (`wilderness_stray`) | **Alignment:** Chaotic Good
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Prowling Beasts (`beast`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Coastal Fishing Hut
- **Company:** Tide Lion

### Backstory

They pulled Halbrec from the wreckage of a splintered caravel on the western shoals during a storm that drowned three ports. He had no memories of his homeland, no passport, and his veins ran cold as deep seawater. Standing guard over his salt-stained body was a spectral feline forged of living surf—a Tide Lion that obeyed his whispered maritime tongue.

Settlers at the fishing hamlets took him in, and in return Halbrec stood in the shallows each evening, spearing marauding reef-sharks and drowned crawlers before they could reach the drying nets. When the coastal waters began turning black with abyssal seepage, Halbrec took his trident and stepped inland.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Fluid, quiet, and smelling of brine and ozone. Halbrec speaks in maritime metaphors and moves with the rhythmic surge of the breakers. Beside him, his Tide Lion prowls, drowning enemies in crushing waves while Halbrec impales priority targets from behind water barriers.

## 11. The Sleepless Blade

- **Calling / Template:** `emo_swordsman`
- **Rating:** Punishing (Difficulty 4)
- **Background:** Outcast Drifter (`outcast_drifter`) | **Alignment:** Chaotic Neutral
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Goblins & Vermin (`goblin`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Desolate Frontier Gate
- **Company:** Dusk Shadow

### Backstory

Vane never sleeps. Four years ago, while commanding the night watch at Fort Morwen, Vane closed his eyes for ten fatal minutes. In that brief window of darkness, goblin tunnelers breached the cellar, dragging off sixty garrison troops into the abyssal pits. Vane awoke to silent corridors and blood on the flagstones.

Ever since, sleep brings only the screaming faces of his lost company. He wanders the perimeter roads accompanied by a semi-sentient shadow entity born from his guilt. He has fought sixty battles, sustained forty wounds, and actively seeks out the most ruinous conflicts on the continent, hoping that each fight will finally be the one that grants him rest.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Cynical, exhausted, and grimly efficient. Vane fights with reckless abandon, utilizing low-posture draw techniques and shadow steps that exploit enemy blind spots. He does not ask for loyalty and expects none in return; his survival is an unhealed wound he scratches with cold steel.

## 12. The Tattered One

- **Calling / Template:** `wraith`
- **Rating:** Ruinous (Difficulty 5)
- **Background:** Outcast Drifter (`outcast_drifter`) | **Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
- **Ancestral Grudge:** Goblins & Vermin (`goblin`, +10% damage)
- **Starting Hearth:** Desolate Abyssal Gate
- **Company:** Walks alone

### Backstory

It was once an inquisitor of the First Age whose name was deliberately chiseled off every tombstone in the country. Imprisoned in an iron sarcophagus beneath an abyssal gate for unspeakable heresies against the living, it withered into a wraith of shredded black shrouds and pale malice.

When tectonic pressures and forgotten steps cracked the subterranean seals, the Tattered One slipped through the fissure like smoke. It has no heartbeat, no tongue to negotiate with merchants, and no warmth to share at a fire. Settlements shutter their windows and ring panic bells when its silhouette passes on the road. It hungers only for the collapse of all kingdoms and the silence that follows.

### Ethos & Dynamic

Terrifying, non-verbal, and lethally direct. The Tattered One cannot trade, cannot recruit human companions, and receives no quarter from town watchmen. In combat, it drifts across terrain unhindered, draining vital essence and shattering mortal minds with terrifying necrotic bursts.
