# 16.12 — Tower of God

**Co-founder's list** · Korean manhwa, SIU · **Brief as given:** *tower buildings, tower floors being vastly different and ranging in size*
**Serves:** `GT8`–`GT14`, `LN2`, `LN9`, `SK9`, `AR3`, `PW4`, `PW6`, `RU12`, `KN6`, `MX8`
**Checked against:** Tower of God Fandom (Structure of the Tower, Floors, Shinsu), Anime News Network primer

## What it is

The Tower is the world. 135 confirmed floors, of which 134 have been conquered. Each floor divides
into three: the **Inner Tower**, a test-driven ascent zone; the **Outer Tower**, a residential area
roughly the size of the Americas with its own cultures, environments and ecosystems; and the
**Middle Area** bridging them. Each floor has a **Guardian/Administrator** controlling the floor's
**shinsu**, and a **Ruler** governing under the Guardian's permission. Shinsu substitutes for oxygen
and water — everyone inside breathes it — and to manipulate it you need a **contract with that
floor's Administrator**, which carries terms you can break. Density rises with each floor; low
concentrations behave like gas, high ones like liquid.

Two structural facts are worth more than all of that. **The 43rd floor's Guardian was killed**, so
it has no Ruler and shinsu manipulation does not reliably work there. And **many Outer Tower
residents don't know the Inner Tower exists.**

## Mechanisms worth taking

1. **Each floor is a world, not a level.** Continent-sized, own ecology, own culture, own climate.
2. **Permission, not ability.** Power requires a contract with the floor's administrator, and the contract has terms.
3. **One floor is broken.** A dead administrator leaves a floor where the rules don't apply, and the world has simply absorbed that.
4. **Most people never climb.** The ascent is a minority activity happening above a population that lives ordinary lives.
5. **Two power structures per floor** — the one that rules and the one that permits — and they are not the same.

## Candidates

### `GT9` / `GT10` — are the ten floors alike, and what does a floor look like?
**Candidate:** each floor is **its own place** — its own weather, its own ecology, its own size — and the only thing they share is that something at the top of each one is in charge. Floor scale varies wildly; one is a corridor, one is a country.
**Why it fits:** it makes the Tower worth ten distinct art passes instead of ten palette swaps, which is a `D`-level decision the repo hasn't spent yet.
**Costs:** this is the most expensive candidate in either list. Ten unique floors is ten times the content of one floor with a tint ramp. Mark it honestly and consider three distinct floors plus seven variations.

### `PW4` / `PW6` — where does grammar-power come from, and can it be revoked?
**Candidate:** doctrine works **by permission**, and the permission is floor-local. What you can do on floor three you cannot do on floor seven until something there agrees.
**Falls out:** `PW6` — yes, revocable, and there is a known way to break the terms. `KN9` (can doctrine be wrong) — a book can be perfectly correct and simply not apply where you're standing.
**Costs:** commits `PW2` to "something grants it," which forecloses WATF's reading of the grammar as an impersonal language. Direct collision with **16.1**; ledger item.

### `GT14` — what is at the bottom of the deep?
**Candidate:** a floor where **the thing in charge is dead.** No permission is granted there, no doctrine works, and everyone has quietly agreed not to talk about it.
**Why it fits:** it is the single best answer available to `FR8` (one thing never explained) because the *absence* is the content. Nothing has to be invented — something has to be missing.

### `RU12` / `KN6` — do ordinary people know what the Tower is?
**Candidate:** **no.** Most people in the world have never seen a gate and don't believe the Tower has ten floors. Delvers are a rumoured profession to two-thirds of the map.
**Falls out:** `NW4` (what does an ordinary person believe about gates) and `DV4` (what four armed strangers look like) both get sharper — they look like liars.

### `AR3` — what does gate architecture look like?
**Candidate:** the gate is a **building**, not a hole. Someone built the approach, and it predates everyone.

## Ambiguity, and how to resolve it

- **The floor size figure is contested across wikis** — "the Americas" on one, "North America" on another, producing estimates from 6.5× to 11× Earth. Irrelevant to you, and instructive: the number is unverifiable in-universe too. Answer `LN2` with a *disagreement* rather than a figure.
- **Guardian, Administrator and Ruler are used loosely** in fan sources and sometimes interchangeably. The distinction that matters is the one Earth Kings can use: **who permits** versus **who governs**. Keep those two separate and ignore the vocabulary.
- **Almost nothing is known about outside the Tower**, deliberately and after 500+ chapters. That is the model for `BN` — the fence is a place you never go, not a place with a wall.

## What not to take

The tests. Tower of God's Inner Tower is a sequence of authored set-piece exams with named
administrators and rules. Earth Kings' Tower is procedural delves, and importing a test structure
would replace the generator with content you have to write ten times.
