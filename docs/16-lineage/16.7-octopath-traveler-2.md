# 16.7 — Octopath Traveler 2

**Tier B** · Square Enix / Acquire, 2023 · **Serves:** `SK7`, `SK8`, `DY1`–`DY12`, `AR5`, `CH14`, `LP27`, `LP28`, `MX14`, `MX16`, `NW6`
The reference for **day/night as two rulesets** and for **parallel histories that barely touch**.

## What it is

Eight protagonists with separate stories that share a world and almost never share a scene. Each
has **Path Actions** — ways of interacting with any NPC in the world, split by time of day: one set
at day (ask, hire, waylay) and a different set at night (bribe, ambush, entice). Every ordinary
townsperson carries information, a possession, and a level. Time of day is player-toggleable and
changes who is awake, what shops do, and what is possible.

## Mechanisms worth taking

1. **Night is a different ruleset, not a filter.** Different people awake, different interactions available, different prices.
2. **Every NPC is a small interactive object** — has something to say, something to take, something to be recruited for.
3. **Eight histories that don't converge** are more interesting than one that does, right up until the end.
4. **Towns are legible at a glance** — you can read what a settlement is for from its silhouette and its people.
5. **The world does not wait for the protagonist to be relevant.** Most of it is somebody else's business.

## Candidates

### `SK8` — is darkness dangerous on its own, or is night just dimmer?
**Candidate:** **night is a different set of rules.** Different encounters, different people awake, different prices, different things possible at the same door.
**Falls out:** `SK7` (weather beyond season) gets a cheap strong answer — the day cycle is the weather that matters. `DY2` (what do people drink and where) becomes a *night* answer: the alehouse is where hires happen because that's who's awake.
**Costs:** the repo flips seasons globally at a step count (`CL2`) and prices everything in steps. A day cycle is a second clock, and `SK6` (what is a step) has to absorb it. Real engine work — mark it honestly.

### `DY1` / `NW6` — an ordinary day, and what an ordinary person knows
**Candidate:** every villager knows **one thing worth hearing and has one thing worth taking.** That is their whole characterisation, and it is enough.
**Why it fits:** it turns `DY1`–`DY12` from a worldbuilding essay into a content format the build can actually consume, and it gives `MN8` (what should an NPC want by default) a default that doesn't need an LLM: to keep the one thing.

### `CH14` / `MX16` / `LP28` — where do the other fifteen stand?
**Candidate:** **somewhere that is their own business**, doing it whether or not you arrive. You meet them mid-story, not waiting.
**Falls out:** `LP28` — meeting them tells you a *fragment* of their history, visible from what they're doing, never the whole. `MX14` — the party screen shows what they'd tell a stranger.
**Costs:** requires each of the fifteen to have an activity, which is `CH14` answered sixteen times. It is the cheapest of the Part VI questions to answer and a good one to run first.

### `AR5` / `DY8` — building material and what a place smells like
**Candidate:** lean on the site kinds the repo already has — stilt huts, cairn huts, orchard huts, pine longhouses, dune serais. Each one already implies a climate, a material, a trade and a smell. Answer `AR5` by **reading what's in `data/` and confirming it**, per the book's `(repo:)` rule.

## Ambiguity, and how to resolve it

- **Octopath's eight stories famously don't converge** until a late optional chapter, and opinion splits on whether that's elegant or a failure. For Earth Kings this is settled by `SM5`: if the next run is the same world later, non-convergence is correct, because convergence happens *across* runs instead of within one.
- **Path Actions have a success/failure and reputation cost** that gets fiddly in play. Take the day/night *split*, not the percentage system — Earth Kings already has standing and prices moving ±30%, and a second reputation currency would collide with it.
- **OT2 is a sequel with a different setting** (industrialising, firearms) from OT1's medieval-fantasy. Where you cite Octopath, specify which — the first game is the closer tonal match to Earth Kings, the second is the better systems donor.

## What not to take

The eight-chapter-per-character structure. It is a container for authored story, and Earth Kings'
Part VII deliberately separates authored history from the character it gets cast onto (`LP4`,
`LP25`). Importing the chapter arc would quietly undo the pool design.
