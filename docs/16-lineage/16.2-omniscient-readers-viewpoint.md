# 16.2 — Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint

**Tier A** · Korean web novel / manhwa, sing N shong · **Serves:** `SF1`–`SF3`, `SM5`, `SM10`, `MX11`, `MX20`, `CO1`, `NW7`, `NW9`
**Checked against:** ORV fandom wiki (Star Stream, Scenario, Constellation pages), TV Tropes character index, VS Battles terminology blog
**Already named** in the repo's lineage doc as an influence on `SF3`.

## What it is

Earth falls under the **Star Stream**, a broadcasting system run by dokkaebi that relays humans'
struggles to **constellations** — ascended beings, mostly mythic and historical figures, who watch
as an audience and sponsor favourites. Participants are **incarnations**, forced through numbered
**scenarios** graded F to S with defined clear conditions and time limits. Kim Dokja's advantage is
that he read the novel this world is running on. Constellations become constellations by
accumulating **stories**; a constellation's true name is its vulnerability, so they wear
**modifiers** instead — conceptual titles that summarise them without exposing them.

## Mechanisms worth taking

1. **The world is legible because it was written first.** The advantage isn't foresight, it's knowing the shape.
2. **Reputation is literally power.** Accumulated story makes a being stronger and richer; a popular myth outguns an obscure one.
3. **A true name is a weakness; a title is armour.** Everyone powerful goes by an epithet.
4. **Watchers are a real, in-world, paying audience** — not a metaphor for the player.
5. **Stories outlive their subjects** and become entities in their own right.

## Candidates

### `SF3` — is anyone watching the run from inside the fiction?
**Candidate:** yes, and exactly one. Not a god and not an audience — a **reader**, who knows the shape and not the outcome.
**Why it fits:** the repo already has `annals.gd` writing deeds down and `news.gd` walking them. A single reader is the cheapest possible answer: it needs no new system, only a voice.
**Costs:** commits `FR10` (who tells this world's story to itself) to something non-human, which forecloses "chroniclers and drunks."

### `SM5` / `MX11` — is the next run the same world, later?
**Candidate:** **yes**, and what the last run did survives as *text* — half-remembered, distorted, attributed to the wrong person.
**Falls out:** `SM6`/`MX12` — the Museum is a library the next run can walk into and read about itself, badly. `HS3` (how far back does reliable memory go) becomes "one run."
**Costs:** this is the largest single decision in the project and it forecloses the clean-reset reading. Do not let it land on a `[M]`.

### `KI8` / `NW9` — naming, titles, and fame
**Candidate:** everyone of consequence carries an epithet, and the real name is the private thing. "The Sworn Blade," "The Hedge Priest" are not flavour — they are what the world knows and what news can carry.
**Why it fits:** `NW1`–`NW3` already model news degrading over distance. An epithet survives a hundred retellings; a name doesn't. This makes `NW3` (can someone lie about you faster than the truth) mechanically obvious.

### `NW7` — who remembers the dead, and for how long?
**Candidate:** a deed accumulates until the person becomes the deed. Memorials in `memorials.json` are not for people, they're for stories that got large enough to need somewhere to sit.

### `CO1` — what does the voice say?
**Candidate:** the voice is reading, not narrating. One line, present tense, about someone the player hasn't met yet and will.

### `MX20` — can you meet a character whose temper you rolled?
**Candidate:** yes. They are a worse version of you and do not know it.

## Ambiguity, and how to resolve it

- **ORV's cosmology is openly incomplete.** The wikis say so directly — the novel never delivers a full explanation and hints the structure is larger than what's shown. Treat that as the *model*: `FR8` demands one thing stay unexplained, and ORV proves a world can be enormously specific at the scenario layer while permanently vague at the top.
- **The dokkaebi/constellation/Star Stream hierarchy does not port.** Earth Kings has no economy of watchers, no coins bought from a shop, no sponsorship. If a candidate needs two tiers of audience, it has stopped being ORV's mechanism and started being its plot.
- **Contested among fans:** how much Kim Dokja's foreknowledge actually helps once the timeline diverges. Useful ambiguity — it is the honest answer to `SF7` (is prophecy accurate, does knowing change it): it was accurate until it was used.

## What not to take

The scenario window. Numbered challenges with clear conditions and reward listings is a HUD the
Question Book explicitly worries about (`SM9`, `SF4`). Earth Kings already decided the world moves
on a step clock rather than a quest log — an ORV-style scenario overlay would undo that.
