# 30 — Questions for the founders

Written 2026-10-16 from `main` after #170 (build `9c64cb3` on itch). These are the questions only
`dougie` (Will) or `doug-md` (Ewan) can answer: what the game is, whose words are in it, what it
costs, and what goes public. Anything a persona meeting can vote on under the standing rule (meeting
four, 2026-10-15) is left off.

**How to answer.** Read a question's number aloud, then talk. A long voice note each, or a call
together, both work. Say "default" to take the default, or "skip" to leave it open; a question nobody
answers keeps its default and nothing changes. Each answer is written up the usual way: the
transcript in [worldbuilding/voice-notes](worldbuilding/voice-notes/), the row it settles struck
through on the agenda ([answers.md](worldbuilding/answers.md#what-is-still-to-decide)), and a numbered
decision in [06](06-decisions.md) ([D164](06-decisions.md)). An answered question is struck through
here with its answer, never deleted; a new founder-only question is added in the pull request that
raises it.

**Who.** **Will**, **Ewan**, or **Both** (talk it through together, or each give your view).

Twenty-four questions: nine for Will, six for Ewan, nine for both. Will's GitHub to-dos, which are not
questions, are at the end.

## 2. Story and the sixteen

**6. Is "Ascend" on the death screen the same thing as the quest "Ascend to heaven"?** (Both)

any and say: keep as yours, or rewrite (and which).
_Why:_ they are the story a new player meets first, and they are still **Inferred** (agenda 82).
_Default:_ they stay as working drafts.
