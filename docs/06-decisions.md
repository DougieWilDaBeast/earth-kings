# 06 — Decisions

Append-only. When a design question is settled, record it here with the date and the reasoning.
Never edit a past entry — supersede it with a new one.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Date       | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| D01 | **Eras are retired.** The world advances by _steps_ taken while walking, not by generational eras.                                                                                                                                                                              | 2026-08-27 | The source project was a simulation watched in generational batches. This is a game you play from inside. A player walking the map is the only clock it needs.                                                                                     |
| D02 | **Native Godot, local-only.** No server, no API keys, no network for the core game.                                                                                                                                                                                             | 2026-08-27 | It has to be playable this weekend, on any machine, offline. The server was the thing that made the original hard to hand to someone.                                                                                                              |
| D03 | **LLM minds are deferred behind a seam, not designed out.** AI returns plans; only the engine mutates state.                                                                                                                                                                    | 2026-08-27 | Keeps the door open without paying for it now. The invariant costs nothing today and is expensive to retrofit.                                                                                                                                     |
| D04 | **`Character` and `Unit` are separate types.** Characters persist; Units are battle puppets.                                                                                                                                                                                    | 2026-08-27 | Without this split, progression and permadeath end up tangled in the battle scene and nothing is testable headlessly.                                                                                                                              |
| D05 | ~~**Falling in battle is not death — yet.** A downed party member is dragged out at 25% max HP.~~ **Superseded by D11.**                                                                                                                                                        | 2026-08-27 | Inherited from the tactics core. Permadeath is a pillar, so this was always a placeholder.                                                                                                                                                         |
| D06 | **Skill trees are generated, never authored.** Themes × effect archetypes × rungs, from a hidden grammar.                                                                                                                                                                       | 2026-08-27 | Discovering a power nobody wrote is the hook. Authored trees would be finite and knowable.                                                                                                                                                         |
| D07 | **Doctrine is per-character and decays.** Nothing is inherited; unused knowledge fades after 900 steps.                                                                                                                                                                         | 2026-08-27 | Makes the Library a destination and knowledge a resource you spend effort maintaining, rather than a checkbox.                                                                                                                                     |
| D08 | **Gate rank is set by distance from the Tower**, with jitter.                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | 2026-08-27 | Gives the map a natural difficulty gradient without hand-placing anything, and makes the Tower feel like what it is.                                                                                                                               |
| D09 | **`World.rng` is the single source of world randomness.** Battle-time variance is deliberately not seeded.                                                                                                                                                                      | 2026-08-27 | Same seed, same world — needed for testing and for sharing a world. Reproducible damage rolls would just feel wrong.                                                                                                                               |
| D10 | **Two-player means passing the pad**, one character, one world.                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | 2026-08-27 | Cheapest thing that works this weekend. Hotseat and PvP duels are not ruled out later.                                                                                                                                                             |
| D11 | **Death is the default; survival is earned.** Supersedes D05. A fallen character rolls their _graces_ in order — charm, rescue, lore, ground, luck, capture — and the first to land decides both whether they live and the reason why. Nothing lands, they die.                 | 2026-08-27 | A flat 25% drag-out made permadeath a lie. Making survival come from something you _brought_ — an item, an ally still standing, a book you read, the ground you fell on — means the reason a character lived is always a specific, tellable thing. |
| D12 | **The player picks their class at level 2.** The world waits (`pending_class_choice`); NPCs settle into one on their own.                                                                                                                                                       | 2026-08-27 | The one irreversible build decision in the game should be the player's. NPCs cannot wait for a prompt nobody will answer.                                                                                                                          |
| D13 | **The run ends when the party is gone.** Losing everyone emits `run_ended` and returns to the title.                                                                                                                                                                            | 2026-08-27 | Permadeath that leaves you walking an empty world is not permadeath, it is a bug. Whether the save survives the run is still open — see Q8.                                                                                                        |
| D14 | ~~D13~~ **The run follows the _player character_, not the party.** Companions can all die and the story continues; when the player falls, it stops. **Supersedes D13.**                                                                                                         | 2026-08-27 | Parties are temporary — every adventurer is looking out for themselves. Some of them do not get to see the end of it, and that is the point.                                                                                                       |
| D15 | **A shut gate never reopens. An open one left too long _breaks_** — what is behind it piles up and comes out. One roll per `break_after_steps` window; a failed roll resets the clock. A broken gate raises local danger by 25pp and enemy level by 3.                          | 2026-08-27 | Clearing a gate should be worth something permanent. The pressure comes from neglect, not from respawns — the world would rather you cleared them first. Usually.                                                                                  |
| D16 | **The Tower is finite: 10 floors, with a reward on every one.** Gold each floor, doctrine every 3rd, a generated skill tree every 4th. What happens at the top is _not decided yet_ — `tower_topped` flags it.                                                                  | 2026-08-27 | An endless climb has no shape. A finite one is a goal you can actually reach; the ending can be designed later without changing the climb.                                                                                                         |
| D17 | **A complete Codex makes you formidable.** Understanding raises the power of every generated tree (+20% at 100%), and a full Codex unlocks _choosing_ the theme instead of being handed one.                                                                                    | 2026-08-27 | Cataloguing the grammar should pay off along the journey, not just as a number on a screen. Discovery becomes craft.                                                                                                                               |
| D18 | **Captives can be bought, fought for, or lost.** A ransom and a deadline are set when they are taken; walking to where they are held offers the price. Miss the deadline and they are moved on — or sold, and gone.                                                             | 2026-08-27 | Nobody waits forever for their friends. A captive who is guaranteed to be waiting is not really in danger.                                                                                                                                         |
| D19 | **Gold is the standard of trade** — weapons, armour, consumables, hires and ransoms all price in it.                                                                                                                                                                            | 2026-08-27 | A currency with no sink is a score. Every other open system needed a price attached anyway.                                                                                                                                                        |
| D20 | **You can recruit, and you can part ways.** Settlements offer a hire for gold; anyone but the player can be dismissed. `MAX_PARTY` is 4.                                                                                                                                        | 2026-08-27 | Permadeath without replacement is a countdown. Recruiting turns a loss into a decision about who walks with you next.                                                                                                                              |
| D21 | **Save-scumming is allowed.** Save/load stays free; reloading after a death is left to the player's conscience.                                                                                                                                                                 | 2026-08-27 | It is a normal way people get around a mechanic they find frightening, and policing it costs more than it is worth. To be revisited.                                                                                                               |
| D22 | **A fight is settled only once it is won.** Gates shut, Tower floors, town spoils and rescues are handed to [`_begin_battle`](../src/world/world_scene.gd) as a pending outcome and applied on the way back, never before.                                                      | 2026-08-30 | The scene swap is deferred, so everything written after the call used to run first — you kept the gate, the gold and the floor whether you won or lost. One-way doors that swing shut regardless of the fight are not doors.                       |
| D23 | **A thread runs out of patience, it never cancels.** A stage may carry a `deadline` in steps and a `goto`; when it lapses the `instead` effects run and the thread goes somewhere worse. See [08 — Threads](08-threads.md).                                                     | 2026-09-01 | A thread that expires quietly is a quest log with extra steps. A thread that resolves badly is a one-way door, which is what the rest of the game is made of.                                                                                      |
| D24 | **Charms are earned through gate delves and deep dungeons.** Intermediate floors of gates roll charms carried by fallen delvers. Gate delves are the canonical source for finding death-defying charms.                                                                         | 2026-09-04 | Buying charms in shops made survival purely transactional; earning charms deep in perilous gates makes them hard-won relics that reward delving into danger.                                                                                       |
| D25 | **The Spire Apex concludes in victory.** Floor 10 fields an apex battle against a Spire Archon and elite minions. Winning clears the hoard into the purse, awards max renown and hearth vigour, and lets the player enshrine their victory in the Museum or continue exploring. | 2026-09-04 | A 10-floor climb needs an authored climax and a victorious conclusion that rewards the run rather than leaving the conquest as a dead end.                                                                                                         |
| D26 | **New rifts awaken under continental pressure.** Shut gates stay shut (D15), but shutting multiple gates strains subterranean seals and ignites story threads (`the_deep_breach`) that tear open new abyssal rifts in remote regions.                                           | 2026-09-04 | Preserves D15's rule that cleared gates never un-clear while ensuring the endgame world remains alive with emerging crises.                                                                                                                        |
| D27 | **Tower ascents advance the continent clock.** Each floor scaled in the Tower advances world time by one upkeep interval (30 steps), stepping prowlers, paying trade routes, and ticking threads.                                                                               | 2026-09-04 | Climbing the Spire takes genuine time; the world outside moves on rather than freezing while you fight.                                                                                                                                            |
| D28 | **Sixteen starting characters, one per temper.** The roster is a designed grid — four preference pairs, sixteen lives — replacing the twelve hand-picked founders. Each character carries four small leans, one per letter, so nobody is strictly stronger. | 2026-09-13 | Twelve heroes were twelve characters somebody liked, with no answer to "why these". A grid says what a new character has to be *for* before anyone writes a word of them, and the four axes are a shape players already read without a manual. |
| D29 | **The lead is answered for, not browsed.** `New Game` asks four questions and names the character; the full picker stays reachable behind _Show me all sixteen_. A temper nobody has been written for says so and falls back to the picker. | 2026-09-13 | Being handed a life you did not shop for is the same bargain as the rest of the game — one-way doors, no version of the world you liked better. The fallback is not a hedge: sixteen characters are written one at a time, and the build has to stay playable the whole way through. |
| D30 | **History is authored in pools and cast late.** Backgrounds, grudges, hearths, creeds and oaths are written with no character in mind, marked with every character they could fit, and attached in a casting session. The five hardcoded backgrounds and the nine-point alignment grid are retired; creeds replace alignment and compute chemistry from tags rather than a table. | 2026-09-13 | A history written *for* a character can only confirm what was already decided about them. Written on its own it has to be interesting first — and then it fits three people, and choosing is a real decision. Uncast pieces are never wasted: they become the rumours, NPCs and false books the world is short of. |
| D31 | **The sixteen fell.** The sixteen starting characters are demigods thrown down from a heavenly place by a disaster. They lose their memories, land at random, look as they did above but pass for ordinary people, and share one mark that lets them recognise each other. The multiverse splits into sixteen at the fall — one world per character — and the other fifteen in a lead's world are copies. | 2026-09-24 | It answers `FR4` — the Earth Kings are the sixteen — and gives every one of the sixteen the same reason to be on the road, which D28's grid did not. It also turns the lead's lost memory into the player's first discovery rather than an amnesia trope: the player saw the fall, the lead did not. See [17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md) and [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md). |
| D32 | **A lead's death ends their world; the next run is another world.** The camera watches the world be destroyed and it never returns. The player then plays another surviving one of the sixteen, in that one's own sibling world, **from level 1**. Sixteen worlds are sixteen chances. Builds on D14 — the run still follows the lead — and answers `SM5` and `MX11`. | 2026-09-24 | Makes pillar 4 as large as it can be: the one-way door is a whole world. `dougie` proposed carrying progress across; `doug-md` argued the game should not be easy and the work belongs to the character who did it, and that was agreed. Knowledge carries because the sibling worlds tend to play out alike. See [17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md) and [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md). |
| D33 | **What carries between worlds is knowledge, a ghost and a weapon — never power.** A dead lead is met as a ghost by meditating at a campfire, sharing what their saved journal holds as far as temper and rapport allow. Their signature god-tier weapon crash-lands somewhere in the next world, unproficient to anyone and takeable by others; finding it opens the ghost fully. Neither arrives until the new lead has got as far as the old one did. Every five Tower floors the census shows how many of the sixteen are alive. | 2026-09-24 | Answers `MX13` without betraying pillar 4: nothing is easier, it is better informed. It makes the journal worth keeping, rewards exploration with a hunt for the weapon, and turns earlier deaths into characters the player talks to. See [17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md) and [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md). |
| D34 | **One Tower per world, climbed in chapters of five floors.** Only the lead's party goes in. Every floor cleared sends the party back out into the world. Every five floors the world changes, and the next chapter needs something from the world — an item, a gate cleared, a quest done. The top is not the end: it opens the contest between the surviving leads, each at their most powerful, with time stopped until all have finished. The number of floors is **not decided**; D16's ten stand until it is. Supersedes D25 as the end of the game; D25's floor-10 fight stands. | 2026-09-24 | The Tower tests the player (`GT1`); tying its chapters to the world is what makes the world worth walking. A climb that neglects the world arrives weaker into a harder one. A hundred floors was floated and doubted in the same session, so the count is left open rather than guessed. See [17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md) and [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md). |
| D35 | **A gate tests the world; you cannot leave one until it is beaten.** Inside is a whole other world of any size with an objective, sometimes timed. Any number can go in; an S-rank gate is a national threat that gathers kingdoms and armies. The Adventurers Guild manages gates. Supersedes M5's retreat-and-keep-the-floors when built. | 2026-09-24 | Gives gates a purpose distinct from the Tower (`GT1`, `GT2`, `OT8`, `DV2`) and makes going into one a commitment rather than a probe. See [17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md) and [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md). |
| D36 | **The fight moves to real time with pause.** Point-and-click movement on the tile grid, automatic basic attacks, four active abilities on Q W E R plus passives, an auto-ability option, pause and speed control at any time — after _Dungeon Settlers_ — with every attack animated after _Fire Emblem: Three Houses_. **Nothing is built before a research deep dive** into _Dungeon Settlers_' combat. Supersedes the charge-time turn order and the squad phase once a prototype has beaten the current battle. | 2026-09-24 | `BN6`: the founders' shared fear is fights that are the same moves every time. Four active slots force a decision before the fight instead of a menu scroll during it, and pause keeps it tactical. The research-first rule is because this rewrites most of `src/battle/`. See [18](18-combat-direction.md). |
| D37 | **Experience comes from the first kill of each kind of enemy; skill comes from use.** Per character: the last hit takes the experience, and assists on a kind add up to it (five agreed, ten for grunts also said). Bosses give everyone involved their experience. Proficiency in each weapon and kind of fighting rises by using it. Supersedes M1's experience to whoever lands any killing blow. | 2026-09-24 | Pillar 1 said power is scarce and not farmed; this makes it so. It is also the oldest idea in the project — 07 lists "XP only for first clears" in the design DNA that predates CHRONICLE — and the reason to go somewhere new is that there is somebody new to beat. See [18](18-combat-direction.md#getting-stronger). |
| D38 | **Companions can be sent away on jobs.** From the job log — quests and rumours picked up in play — party members can be dispatched to gather, meet someone, do a task or join a gate raid. They travel the map in real time while the lead does something else, report back, and may not come back. The party starts capped at four and grows to six; how it grows is open. Supersedes D20's fixed `MAX_PARTY` of 4 when built. | 2026-09-24 | A partial answer to `SM8`: a hire matters because they were trusted with something while you were elsewhere. It also gives the living world a use for the party's spare hands. |
| D39 | **Names are regional, and everyone has an earned one.** The sixteen carry fixed god-style names in a Roman and Greek register. Everyone else's names follow their region — strong, eccentric forms among the nobility near an imperial centre, shortened stems farther out — and everyone has a real name and a title won by deeds, by which renown travels. | 2026-09-24 | `KI8`, `CH1`, `NW9`. A naming rule is cheap to write and makes every generated NPC sound like they come from somewhere. See [17](17-the-sixteen-worlds.md) and [joint session 1](worldbuilding/voice-notes/2026-09-24-joint-session-1.md). |
| D40 | **Dark fantasy, darkening as it goes.** Not repetitive, not all laughs, and never held up by dialogue. | 2026-09-24 | `BN3`, `BN6`. Said by both founders; the fence every later tone call is checked against. |

## Open questions

Discuss before implementing the affected component.

## Answered

- **Q11 — Does the world ever gain new gates?** → [D26](#). Cleared gates never reopen, but deep
  continental pressure causes new abyssal rifts to tear open via story threads.
- **Q15 — Do threads survive the Tower?** → [D27](#). Yes. Each floor climbed advances the
  continent by one upkeep interval (30 steps), ticking threads and upkeep.
- **Q12 — What is at the top of the Tower?** → [D25](#). The Spire Apex: a climactic final battle
  against an Archon and guardians. Victory claims the hoard, grants ascension, and allows the run
  to conclude in triumph at the Museum.
- **Q7 — Where do charms come from?** → [D24](#). Uncovered on intermediate floors of gate delves,
  carried by delvers who came before.
- **Q13 — Can a thread fail?** → [D23](#). No thread ever cancels, but a stage may carry a
  `deadline` in steps that routes to a worse stage rather than to nothing.
- **Q14 — Does the player ever see the threads they are in?** → No. The party screen keeps showing
  accepted errands, because those are promises the player made. A thread is something happening to
  them, and they find out where everything else is found out — on the ground.

- **Q1 — Where does permadeath actually bite?** → [D11](#). Everywhere. Death is the default
  outcome of falling and only a named grace prevents it.
- **Q2 — Does the player pick their class at level 2?** → [D12](#). The player picks.
- **Q3 — Can the Tower be finished?** → [D16](#). Yes, 10 floors, rewards on each. The ending
  itself is deferred to Q12.
- **Q4 — What does a completed Codex unlock?** → [D17](#). Stronger generated trees as
  understanding rises, and deliberate theme choice at 100%.
- **Q5 — Should reopening gates escalate?** → [D15](#). Gates never reopen; neglected ones break.
- **Q6 — How is a captive recovered?** → [D18](#). Bought, fought for, or lost to a deadline.
- **Q8 — Does save/load defeat permadeath?** → [D21](#). Allowed, and revisited later.
- **Q9 — What is gold for?** → [D19](#). Gear, hires and ransoms.
- **Q10 — Can you recruit?** → [D20](#). Yes, and dismiss.
